Sources & data notes

Recipes, ranks, Moogle perks and drops follow Kingdom Hearts Wiki and Destiny Islands' Final Mix guides. Recipes and routes link to their sources. Costs are before reductions; Mythril Crystal uses rank-B Serenity Stone. Serenity drops differ from original KH2.
