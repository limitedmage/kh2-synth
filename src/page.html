Sources & data notes

Recipe quantities, ranks, Moogle perks and enemy drops are based on Kingdom Hearts Wiki, its linked item/material pages, and Destiny Islands' Final Mix guides. Source links accompany recipes and routes. Numbers are base costs, before reductions; Mythril Crystal uses the rank-B Serenity Stone modifier. Final Mix Serenity drops differ from original KH2.

World stages are conservative farming gates, not a walkthrough. Some encounters change during story events; finish the current world episode to restore normal spawns. Chest completion is tracked only with global and per-world switches, including a separate Cavern switch. No chest routes are planned. Optional boss / reward sources are labeled separately from repeatable enemy drops. Drop rates are base rates, before Lucky Lucky.
